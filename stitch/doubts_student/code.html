I'm struggling to understand the integration limits when dealing with periodic pulses in the time domain. Specifically, in Example 4.2, why do we integrate from -T/2 to T/2 instead of 0 to T? Does the choice of origin affect the final phase component of the frequency spectrum?

Dr. Alistair Vance

"Great observation! The choice of integration limits (-T/2 to T/2) is primarily for mathematical symmetry. When the signal is even-symmetric around the origin, it simplifies the complex exponential into a pure cosine term, which makes the computation much cleaner."

"If you use 0 to T, the magnitude spectrum remains identical, but as you suspected, you will get a linear phase shift term in your results. Check the 'Time Shifting Property' in Section 4.5 for the formal proof."
